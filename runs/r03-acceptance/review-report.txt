Failure boundary

Input/logit/gradient rejection does not commit sampling or call Adam; the checked
parameter tensors remain unchanged. Forward/backward can consume dropout RNG.
The separate reviewer probe measured that explicitly. An arbitrary optimizer
failure can partially mutate parameters. A controlled partial optimizer failure
left the immutable sampling state unchanged, and restoring the saved boundary
recovered the exact model and mixed state. Exact retry therefore uses the last
immutable checkpoint. This is the documented boundary, not a transactional
rollback guarantee. Reference: src/chess_rl/training/mixed.py:67 and :110.
